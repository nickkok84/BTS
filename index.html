The story so far, 2013 to 2026
  1. 2013
    Debut. A hip-hop group from a small agency, Big Hit. Debut song: No More Dream.
  2. 2015–16
    Breakthrough. The Most Beautiful Moment in Life series, first music show win (I Need U), then WINGS and their first grand prize in Korea.
  3. 2017–18
    America notices. Billboard Music Award, AMAs debut with DNA, Love Yourself series, first Korean No. 1 album in the US, RM's UN speech.
  4. 2019
    Stadiums. Boy With Luv, two nights at Wembley, and the National Stadium in Singapore.
  5. 2020–21
    Global peak. Dynamite, Life Goes On, Butter and Permission to Dance all hit No. 1 in the US. First Grammy nomination.
  6. 2022–25
    Pause. PROOF anthology, a White House visit, a farewell concert in Busan, then military service. Solo albums from every member.
  7. 2026
    Return. ARIRANG album and title track SWIM both go to No. 1. The world tour reaches Singapore in December.
